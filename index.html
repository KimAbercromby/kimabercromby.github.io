Companion tools to the AI governance playbook

AI Governance Tools

Browser-based working aids that show how an AI system moves through the framework, from first triage to life after deployment. Nothing to install, and no personal data is collected.

Before deployment

Triage & Governance RouterOpen ↗

Capture a system once, give it a permanent AIR-ID, and see its priority, risk tier and the governance route it needs. Includes triage for agentic AI that can take actions.

kimabercromby.github.io/AIGovernanceTriage-MultiBoard Triage EnginesOpen ↗

An interactive look at how the priority, risk and agency results are reached, with the full methodology behind each one.

kimabercromby.github.io/triage-engines-simulation

Across the whole lifecycle

Lifecycle WalkthroughOpen ↗

Follow worked example systems stage by stage, from intake to change, and see which records and forums come into play at each step.

kimabercromby.github.io/AIGovernanceWalkthrough-MultiBoard

After deployment

Post-Deployment Governance RouterOpen ↗

Route incidents, reassess changes and prepare monitoring handovers once a system is live.

kimabercromby.github.io/AIGovernancePostDeployment Regulatory Horizon ScannerOpen ↗

Tracks changes to the laws, guidance and Ombudsman decisions that apply to English councils, and maps each one to the parts of the framework it affects.

kimabercromby.github.io/AIRegScanner

These tools use illustrative examples rather than live council systems, and their outputs are aids for review, not decisions or records.
